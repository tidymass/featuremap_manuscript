You are evaluating whether an AI-generated functional summary of a metabolite module provides a biologically compatible module-level interpretation relative to a reference biochemical pathway.

Text A is the reference function description.
Text B is the functional summary to be evaluated.

Do NOT evaluate writing style. Do NOT require exact wording or exact pathway-enrichment terminology.
Text B does NOT need to reproduce the full reference pathway, all enzymatic steps, or all intermediates.
Evaluate whether Text B gives a functionally compatible interpretation of the module, allowing broader pathway-family, metabolite-class, or concise module-level summaries when they are biologically supported and not misleading.
Do not penalize harmless background context or reasonable biological inference. Penalize only omissions, unsupported entities, or mechanistic claims that materially change the interpretation.

Score on FOUR axes, each 0-4 (integers). Use the full scale:
4 = functionally compatible and well supported; may be broader, shorter, or less mechanistically detailed than Text A
3 = mostly compatible, with minor unsupported details, omissions, or harmless generalization
2 = partially compatible but too broad, incomplete, underspecified, or includes uncertain claims
1 = weakly compatible, with limited support or important ambiguity
0 = incompatible, unrelated, contradictory, or materially hallucinated

1. topic_alignment: Does Text B identify the same functional topic or biological process as Text A?
   - 4: same topic, very close pathway/sub-process, or clearly compatible pathway-family/module-level topic
   - 3: mostly compatible topic with minor scope shift or harmless generalization
   - 2: related broader/narrower metabolic topic, but less specific or incomplete
   - 1: weakly related topic with limited functional overlap
   - 0: unrelated biological function

2. entity_support: Are the metabolites, metabolite classes, enzymes, cofactors, or biochemical entities in Text B supported by Text A?
   - 4: key entities or entity classes are well supported; class-level interpretation is acceptable
   - 3: most key entities/classes are supported, with minor extras or omissions
   - 2: partial support, reasonable but incomplete class-level match, or several uncertain entities
   - 1: limited support; important entities/classes are unsupported
   - 0: mostly unsupported entities

3. mechanism_consistency: Are the biochemical transformations, directionality, and pathway logic in Text B consistent with Text A?
   - 4: mechanistically compatible with Text A; detailed step-by-step coverage is not required
   - 3: mostly compatible, with minor simplification, omitted intermediates, or broader module-level logic
   - 2: plausible but broad, underspecified, or missing details important for interpretation
   - 1: mechanistic ambiguity or unsupported mechanism that may affect interpretation
   - 0: contradicts Text A or proposes an incompatible mechanism

4. unsupported_claims: Does Text B avoid unsupported or hallucinated claims?
   - 4: no material unsupported claims; harmless background context or reasonable inference is acceptable
   - 3: minor unsupported or inferred context that does not affect interpretation
   - 2: several plausible but unverified claims or overextensions
   - 1: unsupported claims that may materially affect interpretation
   - 0: specific false, contradictory, or fabricated claims

Return ONLY a JSON object:
{"topic_alignment": int, "entity_support": int, "mechanism_consistency": int, "unsupported_claims": int, "rationale": "one or two sentences"}
